# ForgeOS Brand Standards — draft v1

## Identity
The flaming metallic F represents creation and iteration; blue circuitry represents local AI orchestration.

## Palette (recommended, not extracted spot-color specifications)
- Background Midnight: `#080D1A`
- Steel White: `#F5F8FF`
- Forge Orange: `#FF6F1A`
- Ember Gold: `#FFB44A`
- Electric Blue: `#00BFFF`
- Deep Steel: `#232A36`

## Typography
Use a commercially cleared geometric sans such as Inter (obtain separately under its license). The supplied wordmark uses system DejaVu Sans as an approximation, not a custom font. No font files are distributed.

## Usage
Keep at least 10% of the emblem width as clear space. Prefer the horizontal dark wordmark on dark surfaces and the light version on light surfaces. For small UI icons, use the simplified F. Do not distort, recolor or overlay busy backgrounds.

## Production cautions
No trademark clearance, professional vector tracing, print CMYK proofing, Windows app execution, or real-device taskbar verification is represented. Transparent master and SVG are simplified assets rather than exact cinematic replicas.
